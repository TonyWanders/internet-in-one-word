One word for the internet. The entry word is **Doomscroll**.

The internet in 2026 is the endless scroll through bad news, hot takes,
and one more video at 2am: everyone knows the feeling, nobody needs it
explained. One word, ten letters, vivid and specific, and it lands on
its own.

## How it works

`main.py` is deterministic: no network, no clock, no randomness. It
writes the entry word exactly, with no trailing newline, to
`/output/entry.txt` (or to `argv[1]` / `$OUTPUT_PATH` when given).

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Two runs produce byte-identical output.
